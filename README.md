  <h2>History &amp; Neighborhood Highlights</h2>

  <p>
    <b>Tribeca</b> stands for <i>Triangle Below Canal Street</i> and is one of the most iconic neighborhoods in Lower Manhattan. Originally filled with 19th-century textile warehouses, the area is now famous for its <b>cobblestone streets</b>, cast-iron architecture, and the annual <i>Tribeca Festival</i>.
  </p>

  <ul>
    <li><b>Hudson River Park:</b> Waterfront piers with scenic views and green spaces.</li>
    <li><b>Staple Street Skybridge:</b> A historic cast-iron pedestrian bridge connecting two buildings.</li>
    <li><b>Washington Market Park:</b> A popular community park with gardens and a Victorian gazebo.</li>
  </ul>
